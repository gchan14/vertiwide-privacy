VertiWide Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

VertiWide is a camera application developed by One-IT Developer. This Privacy Policy explains how VertiWide handles information when you use the app.

1. Information and permissions used by VertiWide

VertiWide may request access to:

  • Camera — used to display the live camera preview and record video.
  • Microphone — used to record audio together with video.

These permissions are used only to provide the app's core recording features.

2. Local processing

VertiWide processes camera frames and microphone audio directly on your device to create the recordings you request. The app is designed around on-device recording and local processing.

3. Video and audio files

Completed recordings are saved to your device's local media storage using Android's media storage system. VertiWide does not provide a built-in cloud upload service in the current version.

You remain in control of the files created by VertiWide. You may view, move, share, or delete them using compatible gallery, file manager, or other apps on your device.

4. Data collection and sharing

The current version of VertiWide does not intentionally transmit your camera recordings, microphone recordings, personal information, precise location, contacts, or other user-created content to One-IT Developer servers.

The current version also does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or a user account system as part of its core build.

5. Accounts and personal information

VertiWide does not require you to create an account or sign in to use the app's core features. The app does not ask for your name, address, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

6. Storage and deletion

Recordings are stored locally on your device. If you want to delete a recording, you can do so through a compatible gallery or file manager application. Uninstalling VertiWide does not necessarily delete media files that were already saved to shared device storage.

7. Children's privacy

VertiWide is a general camera utility and is not specifically designed or directed toward children.

8. Security

VertiWide is designed to minimize remote data handling by performing its core recording workflow locally on the device. No method of electronic storage or software operation can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we aim to limit data exposure by avoiding unnecessary transmission of user recordings.

9. Third-party services

The current version of VertiWide does not use third-party advertising or analytics services in its core build. If future versions add services that change how data is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when VertiWide's features, dependencies, legal requirements, or data practices change. The latest version will be made available at the Privacy Policy URL listed on Google Play.
